Privacy Policy — 1099 Tax Calculator

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1099 Tax Calculator - Gig & SE ("the app") is developed by F-Tech Developers. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information the app collects

The app itself does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline:

  • All calculations, saved estimates, expense records, mileage logs and settings are stored only on your device (in the app's local database).
  • The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.
  • Financial figures you enter (income, expenses, mileage) never leave your device, except when you choose to export or share a report (PDF/CSV) using your own apps.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its locally stored data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address
  • App usage and ad interaction data
  • Diagnostics and performance data

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Settings (Google > Ads).

Tax estimates disclaimer

The app provides estimates only — not tax advice. It is not affiliated with the IRS or any government agency. Tax figures are based on publicly available IRS and state data and simplified state estimate rates; consult a qualified tax professional for your actual filing.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing self-employment income and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
